Published our latest paper on isogeometric topology optimization. This was our fourth (and sort of final) paper on the work that our team (me, Abhinav, Bhagath, and Prof. Rajib) has been working on for the past many years ([Read the history of the project here](https://www.philipluke.com/iga/history/)), where we combined density based topology optimization, multi-patch CAD geometries, adaptive isogeometric analysis using PHT-Splines and structural mechanics (2D, 3D, plates, and shells).

The paper proposes a generalized framework towards continuity and effciency for optimial design using multi-patch IGA.
