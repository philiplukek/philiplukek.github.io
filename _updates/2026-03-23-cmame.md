5th publication from our 'adaptive isogeometric topology optimization' project is now online in CMAME. What's interesting and a matter of pride is that we have a 100% acceptance rate in this project. None of the papers have been rejected and got exactly published at our first preferred journal itself. We thank the editors of seeing the quality in our work and the anonymous reviewers for seeing the value in our submitted drafts and strengthening it with constructive suggestions and comments. 

So far we had been working with the SIMP methodology of topology optimization. This time, we tested the versatility of our framework by implementing the reaction-diffusion equation based topology optimization methodology. We combined RDE-TO, Multi-patch NURBS, Adaptive mesh refinement and the GIFT framework in our unified methodology and tested it on 2D and 3D geometries, with excellent optimized topologies and remarkable computational speedup.
